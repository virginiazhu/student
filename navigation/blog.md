## 💬 Reflection on Growth

At the start of the trimester, I often got frustrated when code didn’t work right away. Over time, I developed **coding endurance** — the ability to stay focused and curious instead of giving up quickly. This helped me improve both my **behavior** and **work habits**, since I learned to enjoy the process of figuring things out.

My **technical communication** also improved. I started using proper terms when explaining my reasoning to classmates and felt more confident describing how my code worked.  
Lastly, I became more **collaborative and organized**, keeping track of group work efficiently and supporting others when they ran into challenges.

Overall, I’ve grown into a more patient, independent, and communicative learner who enjoys the challenge of solving problems through code.
